It is positional rather than named (`text=…`) because Hugo refuses to mix positional and named parameters in one shortcode call, and making the common case `{{< abbr term="NUT" >}}` was the worse trade. Definitions come from data/glossary.yaml (site.Data.glossary), whose entries carry `name`, `description` and an optional `url`. The registry is keyed by the abbreviation, so two expansions of the same letters need two keys — `GC` for Garbage Collection, `GC_GOATCOUNTER` for GoatCounter. The optional `rendered_text` on an entry is what the reader sees instead of that disambiguating key, so the suffix never leaves the file.
