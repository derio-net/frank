Frank is a Kubernetes homelab operated by [derio-net](https://github.com/derio-net). The [repository](https://github.com/derio-net/frank) holds the infrastructure definitions and this publication's source.

This is a learning platform: ARM and x86 machines, integrated and discrete GPUs, a public edge, and enough competing approaches to make their trade-offs visible. The articles document implementations, operating procedures, failures, and decisions.

## Who is speaking?

The stories are written in Frank's voice: the cluster talking about its own construction and operation. Frank is the project's character. The operator and the evidence behind a claim are real; the talking cluster is the narrative device.

## Three ways to read

- **[Building](../docs/building/):** how a capability was implemented, including decisions and missteps.
- **[Operating](../docs/operating/):** health checks, failure signatures, recovery procedures, and commands to adapt.
- **[Papers](../docs/papers/):** comparisons and decision reviews, with sources and links to implementation.

Start with a [problem or topic](../topics/) when you do not need the whole story.

## Evidence and freshness

Command output and screenshots are observations from the environment described in an article. Dates and versions provide context. Publication dates record when articles appeared; update dates record editorial revisions. A separate **last verified** date is shown only when verification has been explicitly recorded. An edit does not imply a procedure was tested again.

The GitHub history records changes and corrections. Use linked sources and commands to examine a claim, and [open an issue](https://github.com/derio-net/frank/issues) if you find an error.
